---
title: Privacy
description: What data Energy Prices NL uses and where it goes.
---

**Short version: the plugin collects no personal data.**

- **No account.** You don't sign in anywhere.
- **What it fetches.** Every 15 minutes the plugin downloads the public electricity prices for today and tomorrow from EnergyZero's public price service (`public.api.energyzero.nl`). The request contains only the date. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that service.
- **What it stores.** Your settings (purchase fee, display options) are stored by the Stream Deck app on your computer. They are not sent anywhere.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no telemetry, no ads.
- **Logs.** The plugin writes a small log file on your computer, used only for troubleshooting. It is never sent anywhere unless you send it to us yourself.

Questions about privacy? See [Support](../../support/).
