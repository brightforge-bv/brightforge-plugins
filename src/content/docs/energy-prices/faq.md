### The key says "Loading…"

The plugin is fetching today's prices. This normally takes a second. If it stays there, check your internet connection.

### The key says "Offline"

The plugin could not reach the price service. It keeps retrying every minute and shows prices as soon as it gets them.

### Tomorrow's keys say "After 13:00"

Tomorrow's day-ahead prices are published early in the afternoon, usually between 13:00 and 14:00. The plugin checks for new prices every 15 minutes, so tomorrow's prices appear shortly after they are published.

### The price differs from my supplier's app

Check these, in order:

1. Is your **purchase fee** right, and **including VAT**? See [Price settings](../price-settings/).
2. Does your supplier's app show the **market price** or the **all-in price**? Switch *Show* to compare.

Small differences of a tenth of a cent come from rounding.

### Why can the price be negative?

When there is more solar and wind power than demand, the market price can drop below zero. With tax and VAT the all-in price is usually still positive, but not always. The chart then draws bars below the zero line.

### Does it work outside the Netherlands?

No. Prices, energy tax and VAT are for the Netherlands only.

### Does it work with gas prices?

Not yet. The plugin shows electricity prices only.

### Does it control my devices?

No. The plugin only shows prices; it does not switch anything.

### Can I use it on several Stream Decks?

Yes. Settings under *Prices* are shared by all keys and dials on all your Stream Decks.
