## 1.0.0

First release.

- **Energy Price key:** price now, next quarter-hour, or the day's lowest, highest or average, for today or tomorrow. Press to switch between figure and chart.
- **Energy Prices dial** for Stream Deck +: browse the day by quarter-hour or hour, switch between today and tomorrow, link dials into one wide chart with a price breakdown.
- **All-in prices** with your purchase fee, energy tax and 21% VAT, or market price only. Cents or euros.
