<Steps>

1. **Install the plugin** from the Elgato Marketplace. It shows up in the Stream Deck app under **Energy Prices NL**.

2. **Drag the Energy Price action** onto a key. It shows the price for the current quarter-hour right away.

3. **Enter your purchase fee.** Select the key and, under *Prices*, fill in your supplier's **Purchase fee** in cents per kWh including VAT, for example `2.0`. You only do this once: it applies to all keys and dials.

4. **Add more keys** for the next quarter-hour, or the day's lowest, highest or average price. See [Keys and dials](../keys-and-dials/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Where do I find my purchase fee?">
Suppliers call it *inkoopvergoeding*, *opslag* or *inkoopkosten*. It is in the tariff sheet of your contract, or in your supplier's app. Make sure you use the amount **including VAT**. Not sure? Leave it empty and the plugin shows market price + energy tax + VAT.
</Aside>

## Stream Deck +

Drag the **Energy Prices Dial** onto one or more dials. Turn on **Link to neighbouring dials** on each dial next to each other to get one wide chart, with the price breakdown on the rightmost dial.
