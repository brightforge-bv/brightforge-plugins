| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Show** | Price now · Next quarter-hour · Lowest of the day · Highest of the day · Average of the day |
| **Day** | Today · Tomorrow. Applies to lowest, highest and average. |

**Press the key** to switch between the figure and a chart of the day. In the chart, past quarter-hours are dimmed, the selected one is white and a small marker shows *now*.

The figure's colour tells you where it sits in the day: **green** for the cheapest third, **amber** for the middle and **red** for the most expensive third.

## Energy Prices dial (Stream Deck +)

<Image class="device-shot" src={dialSingle} alt="One dial: the selected quarter-hour's price above a chart of the day" width={200} densities={[1, 2]} />

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rotate** | Move through the day by quarter-hour. Past midnight it continues into tomorrow, once those prices are known. |
| **Press and rotate** | Move by the hour. |
| **Press** | Back to *now*. |
| **Tap the touch strip** | Switch between today and tomorrow. |

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Link** | Join neighbouring dials that have Link on into one wide chart. |
| **Start on** | Today · Tomorrow (when published). The day shown after a press. |

### Linked dials

Turn on **Link** for two or more dials next to each other. They share one chart, and the rightmost dial shows how the selected price adds up: market price, purchase fee, energy tax and VAT. Rotate any of them to move through the day.
