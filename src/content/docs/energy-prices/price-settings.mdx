The settings under **Prices** are shared: change them on one key or dial and every Energy Prices key and dial follows.

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Purchase fee** | Your supplier's fee on top of the market price, in cents per kWh **including VAT**. Empty means 0. |
| **Show** | **All-in price** (default) · **Market price only** |
| **Unit** | **Cents** (23.4 ct) · **Euros** (€0.234) |

## How the all-in price adds up

```
all-in = (market price + purchase fee + energy tax) × 1.21
```

- **Market price:** the EPEX day-ahead price for that quarter-hour.
- **Purchase fee:** what you entered, converted to excluding VAT.
- **Energy tax:** the Dutch *energiebelasting* per kWh, as published with the prices.
- **VAT:** 21%.

On a Stream Deck +, linked dials show this breakdown for the selected quarter-hour.

<Aside type="note" title="What is not included">
Fixed monthly costs (*vaste leveringskosten*, *netbeheerkosten*) and the annual energy tax refund (*vermindering energiebelasting*) do not depend on when you use power, so they are left out.
</Aside>

## Market price only

Shows the bare EPEX price, without fee, tax or VAT. Labels then get *(mkt)*. Handy if you compare with the price in your supplier's app, which sometimes shows the market price.
